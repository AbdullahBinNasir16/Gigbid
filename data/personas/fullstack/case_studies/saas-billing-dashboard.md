# SaaS billing dashboard

## The problem
The client, a saas company, had a problem: a SaaS team handled upgrades and refunds through support tickets.

## What I built
I built a self-serve billing portal with Stripe subscriptions, invoices and usage metering. The stack was Next.js, TypeScript, Stripe, PostgreSQL and Tailwind CSS.

## Result
The work support tickets about billing fell 64%.
