# Real-time delivery tracking

## The problem
The client, a logistics company, had a problem: dispatchers phoned drivers to find out where parcels were.

## What I built
I built a live map with driver locations, ETAs and customer tracking links. The stack was React, Node.js, WebSockets, Google Maps API and PostgreSQL.

## Result
The work "where is my order" calls fell by half.
