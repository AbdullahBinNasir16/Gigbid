# Real estate listings portal

## The problem
The client, a real estate company, had a problem: an agency's listings were only on third-party portals.

## What I built
I built a listings site with map search, saved searches and lead capture. The stack was Next.js, PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch, Mapbox and TypeScript.

## Result
The work generated 300 qualified leads a month.
