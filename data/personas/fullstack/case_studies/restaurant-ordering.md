# Restaurant online ordering

## The problem
The client, a hospitality company, had a problem: a restaurant group paid 30% commission to delivery apps.

## What I built
I built a branded ordering site with menus, modifiers and kitchen printing. The stack was WordPress, WooCommerce, PHP, JavaScript and Stripe.

## Result
The work moved 40% of orders to the direct channel within six months.
