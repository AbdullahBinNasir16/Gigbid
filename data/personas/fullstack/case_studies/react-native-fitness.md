# Fitness tracking mobile app

## The problem
The client, a health and fitness company, had a problem: a personal trainer tracked client workouts in spreadsheets.

## What I built
I built a cross-platform app with workout plans, progress charts and push notifications. The stack was React Native, TypeScript, Firebase, Expo and Mobile App.

## Result
The work reached 4.7 stars on both stores with 6,000 installs.
