# Heroku to AWS migration

## The problem
The client, a saas company, had a problem: hosting costs grew faster than revenue.

## What I built
I built a containerised move to ECS with Terraform, blue-green deploys and RDS. The stack was AWS, Docker, Terraform, CI/CD and PostgreSQL.

## Result
The work cut hosting cost 45% with zero downtime during cutover.
