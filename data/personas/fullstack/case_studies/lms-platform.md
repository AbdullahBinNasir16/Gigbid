# Learning management platform

## The problem
The client, a education company, had a problem: a training company emailed course PDFs to learners.

## What I built
I built an LMS with video lessons, quizzes and certificates. The stack was Laravel, PHP, Vue.js, MySQL and AWS.

## Result
The work served 9,000 learners in its first year.
