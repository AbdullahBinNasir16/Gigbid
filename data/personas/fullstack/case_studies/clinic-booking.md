# Clinic appointment booking system

## The problem
The client, a healthcare company, had a problem: receptionists booked appointments by phone and paper.

## What I built
I built an online booking system with SMS reminders and a staff calendar. The stack was Django, Python, PostgreSQL, React and Celery.

## Result
The work no-shows dropped 35%.
