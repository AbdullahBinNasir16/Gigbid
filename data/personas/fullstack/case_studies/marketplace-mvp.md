# Two-sided services marketplace MVP

## The problem
The client, a home services company, had a problem: a founder needed a working marketplace in 8 weeks.

## What I built
I built an MVP with listings, booking, chat and reviews. The stack was React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB and Socket.io.

## Result
The work launched on time and onboarded 1,200 providers in the first two months.
