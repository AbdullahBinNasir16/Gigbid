# Retail demand forecasting

## The problem
The client, a retail company, had a problem: stores were over-ordering perishables.

## What I built
I built a weekly SKU-level demand forecast with holiday features. The stack was Time Series, LightGBM, Python, Pandas and Forecasting.

## Result
The work lowered MAPE from 31% to 18% and cut waste by 12% in the pilot stores.
