# Meeting transcription and summarisation

## The problem
The client, a consulting company, had a problem: consultants wrote meeting notes by hand.

## What I built
I built a Whisper transcription and LLM summary service with speaker labels. The stack was Speech Recognition, Whisper, LLM, Python and FastAPI.

## Result
The work notes were ready 2 minutes after each call.
