# Patient message triage classifier

## The problem
The client, a healthcare company, had a problem: a clinic inbox had urgent messages buried among routine ones.

## What I built
I built a de-identified text classifier that routes messages to urgent, routine or admin queues. The stack was NLP, Text Classification, Python, FastAPI and Hugging Face.

## Result
The work urgent messages reached a nurse 3 times faster.
