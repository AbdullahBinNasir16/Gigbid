# MLOps training and deployment pipeline

## The problem
The client, a fintech company, had a problem: data scientists deployed models by copying pickles to servers.

## What I built
I built a CI/CD pipeline with MLflow tracking, model registry and canary deploys on EKS. The stack was MLOps, Docker, Kubernetes, MLflow and AWS.

## Result
The work deployment time went from 2 days to 25 minutes.
