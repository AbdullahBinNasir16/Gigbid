# Manufacturing defect detection

## The problem
The client, a manufacturing company, had a problem: manual inspection missed hairline cracks on a bottling line.

## What I built
I built a YOLO-based detector running on an edge GPU at 30 frames per second. The stack was Computer Vision, PyTorch, YOLO, OpenCV and Python.

## Result
The work raised recall on cracked bottles from 72% to 96%.
