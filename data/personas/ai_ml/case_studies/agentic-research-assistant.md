# Agentic research assistant

## The problem
The client, a consulting company, had a problem: analysts compiled market briefs by hand.

## What I built
I built a LangGraph multi-agent assistant that plans, searches, and drafts briefs with human review. The stack was LangGraph, Agents, Tool Calling, Python and Tavily.

## Result
The work cut brief preparation from 6 hours to 50 minutes.
