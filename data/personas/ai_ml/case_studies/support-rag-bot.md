# Customer support RAG chatbot

## The problem
The client, a e-commerce company, had a problem: support agents answered the same 400 questions every week.

## What I built
I built a retrieval-augmented chatbot over 3,200 help-centre articles with source citations. The stack was RAG, LangChain, Pinecone, OpenAI API and FastAPI.

## Result
The work cut first-response time from 9 hours to 40 seconds and deflected 38% of tickets.
