# Subscription churn prediction

## The problem
The client, a saas company, had a problem: a SaaS company lost customers without warning.

## What I built
I built a churn model with SHAP explanations feeding the CRM. The stack was Machine Learning, XGBoost, scikit-learn, SQL and Python.

## Result
The work AUC 0.86; the retention team saved an estimated $14k MRR in the first quarter.
