# Payment anomaly detection

## The problem
The client, a fintech company, had a problem: card fraud was caught days late.

## What I built
I built a streaming isolation-forest scorer on payment events. The stack was Anomaly Detection, Python, scikit-learn, Kafka and Machine Learning.

## Result
The work flagged 82% of confirmed fraud within 5 minutes at a 1.5% review rate.
