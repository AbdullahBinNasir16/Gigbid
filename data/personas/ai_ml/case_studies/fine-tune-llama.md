# Domain fine-tuning of an open LLM

## The problem
The client, a education company, had a problem: a tutoring company needed answers in its own curriculum style.

## What I built
I built a LoRA fine-tune of an open 8B model on 12,000 curated question-answer pairs. The stack was Fine-tuning, LoRA, Hugging Face, PyTorch and LLM.

## Result
The work human graders preferred it over the base model in 71% of pairwise comparisons.
