# Semantic product search

## The problem
The client, a e-commerce company, had a problem: keyword search returned nothing for natural-language queries.

## What I built
I built hybrid BM25 and embedding search over 80,000 products with a reranker. The stack was Semantic Search, Vector Database, Qdrant, Embeddings and Python.

## Result
The work search-to-cart conversion rose 11%.
