# Invoice data extraction

## The problem
The client, a accounting company, had a problem: an accounting firm keyed invoices by hand.

## What I built
I built an OCR and layout-model pipeline that extracts 12 invoice fields into their ERP. The stack was OCR, Document AI, Python, Tesseract and Pandas.

## Result
The work processing time fell from 6 minutes to 20 seconds per invoice at 97% field accuracy.
