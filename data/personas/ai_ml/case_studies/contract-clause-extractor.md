# Legal contract clause extraction

## The problem
The client, a legal company, had a problem: paralegals spent days tagging clauses in supplier contracts.

## What I built
I built a fine-tuned token classifier that tags 14 clause types in PDFs. The stack was NLP, Hugging Face, Transformers, Python and spaCy.

## Result
The work reached 0.91 F1 on a held-out set and saved about 20 paralegal hours a week.
