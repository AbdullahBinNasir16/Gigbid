# Multilingual FAQ assistant

## The problem
The client, a telecom company, had a problem: customers asked the same questions in Urdu and English.

## What I built
I built a bilingual retrieval assistant with language detection and fallback to human agents. The stack was Chatbot, RAG, Multilingual NLP, Python and LangChain.

## Result
The work handled 55% of conversations without escalation.
