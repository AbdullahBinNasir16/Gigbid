# Operations KPI dashboard in Tableau

## The problem
The client, a logistics company, had a problem: warehouse leads tracked KPIs on whiteboards.

## What I built
I built a Tableau dashboard of pick rates, late shipments and labour hours. The stack was Tableau, SQL, PostgreSQL, Dashboard and KPI.

## Result
The work late shipments fell 15% in the first quarter of use.
