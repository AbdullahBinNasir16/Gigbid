# Customer satisfaction survey analysis

## The problem
The client, a hospitality company, had a problem: a hotel chain had 18,000 survey responses nobody had read.

## What I built
I built a driver analysis with regression and open-text topic coding. The stack was Statistics, Python, Survey Analysis, Excel and Data Visualization.

## Result
The work identified check-in wait as the top detractor; fixing it lifted NPS by 9 points.
