# Marketing attribution analysis

## The problem
The client, a e-commerce company, had a problem: the team could not tell which channels drove revenue.

## What I built
I built a multi-touch attribution model on GA4 BigQuery exports. The stack was Python, SQL, Google Analytics, Statistics and BigQuery.

## Result
The work shifted 20% of spend to the two best channels; ROAS rose 27%.
