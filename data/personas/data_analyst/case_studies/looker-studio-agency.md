# Client reporting for a marketing agency

## The problem
The client, a marketing company, had a problem: an agency built client reports by screenshot every month.

## What I built
I built templated Looker Studio reports for 25 clients from GA4 and ad platforms. The stack was Looker Studio, Google Sheets, Google Analytics, Facebook Ads and Dashboard.

## Result
The work saved the agency about 60 hours a month.
