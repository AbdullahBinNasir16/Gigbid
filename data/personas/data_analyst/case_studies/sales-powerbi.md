# Sales performance dashboard

## The problem
The client, a retail company, had a problem: regional managers waited a week for sales numbers.

## What I built
I built a Power BI dashboard on the sales warehouse with daily refresh and row-level security. The stack was Power BI, DAX, SQL, Excel and Dashboard.

## Result
The work reporting time went from 5 days to same-day; used by 40 managers.
