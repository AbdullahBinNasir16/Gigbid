# Employee attrition analysis

## The problem
The client, a manufacturing company, had a problem: a factory lost a third of new hires in 90 days.

## What I built
I built a survival analysis of attrition by shift, manager and pay band. The stack was Power BI, Python, Statistics, HR Analytics and Excel.

## Result
The work a revised onboarding plan cut 90-day attrition from 33% to 21%.
