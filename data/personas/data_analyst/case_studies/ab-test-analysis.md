# A/B test analysis framework

## The problem
The client, a saas company, had a problem: product managers declared winners after two days.

## What I built
I built a test-readout template with power analysis and sequential testing guardrails. The stack was A/B Testing, Statistics, Python, SQL and Experimentation.

## Result
The work stopped three false-positive launches in its first two months.
