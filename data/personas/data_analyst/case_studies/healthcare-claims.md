# Insurance claims analysis

## The problem
The client, a healthcare company, had a problem: an insurer suspected billing outliers but had no evidence.

## What I built
I built an outlier analysis of 2.1 million claims by provider and procedure. The stack was SQL, Python, Pandas, Data Analysis and Tableau.

## Result
The work flagged 37 providers for review; $410k in overbilling confirmed.
