# CRM data cleanup

## The problem
The client, a real estate company, had a problem: a brokerage CRM held 40,000 contacts with many duplicates.

## What I built
I built a deduplication and standardisation pass with documented rules. The stack was Data Cleaning, Google Sheets, Excel, Data Entry and Deduplication.

## Result
The work removed 11,000 duplicates and fixed 8,000 malformed phone numbers.
