# Competitor price monitoring from licensed feeds

## The problem
The client, a retail company, had a problem: a retailer priced products without knowing the market.

## What I built
I built a weekly price comparison from a licensed price-data feed with matching rules. The stack was Python, Pandas, Data Cleaning, Excel and Price Analysis.

## Result
The work margin rose 2.3 points on matched products.
