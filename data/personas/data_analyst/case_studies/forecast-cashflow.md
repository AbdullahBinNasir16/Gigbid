# Cash-flow forecasting model

## The problem
The client, a wholesale company, had a problem: a wholesaler was surprised by cash shortfalls.

## What I built
I built a 13-week cash-flow forecast combining receivables ageing and seasonality. The stack was Forecasting, Excel, Python, Financial Analysis and Time Series.

## Result
The work forecast error stayed within 6% over two quarters.
