# E-commerce cohort and LTV analysis

## The problem
The client, a e-commerce company, had a problem: a store did not know which customers came back.

## What I built
I built cohort retention and LTV curves by acquisition channel. The stack was SQL, Cohort Analysis, Python, Data Visualization and Shopify.

## Result
The work showed paid social customers had 40% lower LTV; budget was rebalanced.
